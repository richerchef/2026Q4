How Spiral Visualizations Work

Unlike linear timelines where repeating patterns get stretched out horizontally, a Polar Spiral wraps time around a circle.

  • Angle ($\theta$): Represents phase position within a cycle (e.g. Hour of day or Month of year).
  • Radius ($r$): Represents continuous time progression outwards from center.
💡 Finding Periodicities & Alignment: When the selected Cycle Duration matches the true underlying periodicity of the data, repeating spikes will form striking radial spokes or straight rays beaming out from the center!

Use the "Auto-Detect Dominant Cycle" button to let the algorithm automatically search for periodic signals using Fast Fourier Transforms and Autocorrelation.
